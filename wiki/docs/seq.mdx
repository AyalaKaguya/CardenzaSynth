# 2 SEQ

Every track has up to 16 patterns. Each pattern has its own length, 1–256 steps (16 to start with), and the GRID shows it one page of 16 steps at a time. Each step holds up to 8 notes (a chord), each note with its own length, velocity and a small timing offset (6 microsteps per step). SEQ has three pages:

| Page | For |
|---|---|
| <Page name="ROLL" /> / <Page name="STEP" /> note page | writing, editing and recording notes, in two views: ROLL piano roll and STEP, drawn like a drum machine; on a drum track the STEP view is the <Page name="DRUMS" /> drum machine |
| <Page name="PATTERN" /> | choosing, creating and queueing the track's patterns |
| <Page name="TRACK" /> | the track's sequencer switch, the current pattern's length, global swing and BPM |

<Keywords k="sequencer, sequence, pattern, steps, loop, beat, arrangement, 音序器" />

## The note page's two views {#seq-notes}

<Key k="OK" /> is this page's <Icon name="rec" /> **record key**: tap it to switch between the two views, hold it for the RECORD panel. With steps selected a tap on <Key k="OK" /> picks what you edit instead (see [Selecting and editing](#seq-edit)).

<Strip>
  <Step shot="seq-notes" at="footer"><Icon name="roll" /> **ROLL piano roll**: time × pitch, notes drawn as bars. Melodic tracks start here.</Step>
  <Step keys="OK" shot="seq-step-view" at="footer 116,14,113,108"><Icon name="step" /> Tap <Key k="OK" />: **STEP**, a row per note over the steps, and on the right the GRID of the note the pads chose. Drum tracks start here: it is their drum machine.</Step>
  <Step keys="OK" shot="seq-notes" at="footer">Tap again: back to ROLL. The OK strip on screen is <Led state="half" /> in ROLL, <Led state="full" /> in STEP.</Step>
</Strip>

Both views show the same pattern and **take exactly the same keys**; only the drawing differs. Drum tracks and melodic tracks each remember their own view. The 16 GRID keys are the 16 steps of the page on screen (row 1 is steps 1–4, row 2 steps 5–8…):

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| GRID tap | selects that step and keeps it, so your hands are free for the pads; tap it again to let go, tap another step to move there (with SELECT MULTI a tap adds a step) |
| GRID held | the step is selected while its key is down and let go when you release it; steps held together are edited together. Stopped, you hear the step while you hold it |
| PADS held + GRID | the held notes go into that step; if it already holds any of them, they all come out instead |
| PADS with steps selected | the same on the selected steps: a pad's notes (its whole chord) go in, or come out |
| PADS with nothing selected | just play (and record live with REC on) |
| <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> | selected steps: change them; nothing selected: next / previous page of a long pattern |
| <Key k="FN+ADD" /> / <Key k="FN+SUB" /> | selected steps: change them in bigger steps; nothing selected: next / previous pattern |
| <Key k="OK" /> | tap: switch the view (with steps selected: the next edit lane); hold: the RECORD panel |

Nothing moves on by itself: there is no write cursor, and a step you leave alone is a rest.

<Keywords k="view, mode, piano roll, step view, switch view, step keys, matrixos, 视图" />

The page's steps run left to right (a pattern shorter than 16 steps fills the width too), pitch bottom to top. On a melodic track the rows are every key the pads play at the current octave, used or not, black-key rows shaded; notes outside that range widen it (up to 41 rows). On a drum track the rows are the drums in use, by name. The more solid a note, the higher its velocity; the green line is the playhead. The small cells at the bottom: dithered where a step holds notes, white where it is selected, green where the playhead is.

While you hold pads, the rows of their notes light up across the roll (chord tones too), so you see where a tapped step will write. An empty pattern says HOLD A PAD + TAP STEPS.

**Write notes: hold one or more PADS, then tap steps on the GRID.**

<Strip>
  <Step keys="hold r3c1~C" shot="seq-hold-pad">Hold a note (C3, bottom left): its row lights up.</Step>
  <Step keys="hold r3c1~C + G1" shot="seq-hold-tap" at="28,57,15,10">Keep holding and tap GRID 1: C is written into step 1.</Step>
  <Step keys="hold r3c1~C + G5, G9" shot="seq-notes" at="28,110,195,11">Go on with 5, 9…, then switch to another note and tap a few more steps. Tap a step that has the note and it comes out again.</Step>
</Strip>

- **Look at a step**: tap its GRID key; its notes light up fully on the pads, so you see at once what it holds. Tap the same step again to let go. See [Selecting and editing](#seq-edit).
- **Record live**: turn REC on (<Key k="FN+PLAY" /> or the RECORD panel), start playback, then play the pads, see [Play and live recording](#seq-transport).
- With the track's sequencer off (TRACK page) the page says SEQUENCE OFF - SEQ > TRACK.

- **Left**: one row per note (on a melodic track every key the pads play at this octave, the highest at the top, black-key rows shaded; widened for notes outside it, up to 41 rows) over the page's 16 steps. Held notes light their row; the selected steps light their column.
- **Right**: the 4×4 GRID for the note the pads chose last (its name above): its 16 steps, how hard by dither density, the selected steps inverse.
- The keys are those of every note page (see the table in [The note page's two views](#seq-notes)). To write a step at a time without keeping time:

  <Step keys="G2, r3c2~D, r3c3~E" shot="seq-step" at="144,25,26,22">Tap GRID 2: step 2 is selected and stays so. Press D, then E: both go into step 2. Press E again and it comes out.</Step>

- Notes written from the pads get the velocity of SETUP KEYS (VELO) and are one step long; drums go in as hard as their pad's row. Change them with the edit lanes (see [Selecting and editing](#seq-edit)).
- While playing, the step the playhead enters flashes green once and keeps a green outline until the next step; the steps behind it leave no trail.
- VIEW in the FN menu and in the RECORD panel switches the view too.

## Selecting and editing {#seq-edit}

A GRID key **selects** its step, in both views and on drum tracks alike, the way MatrixOS's sequencer does:

- **Tap** (released within 0.25 s, no other key meanwhile): the step stays selected and the hands are free. With **SELECT** ONE (the default) a tap on another step moves the selection there, and a tap on the step selected alone lets it go. With **SELECT** MULTI (on the KEYS page of <Scene name="SETUP" />, see [KEYS](./setup.mdx#setup-keys)) a tap adds a step, and a tap on a selected one takes it out.
- **Hold** (longer, or another key pressed meanwhile): the step is selected only while its key is down. Hold several to edit them together.
- <Key k="ESC" /> clears the selection; turning the page with SELECT ONE clears it too.

<Shot name="seq-selected">

  <KeyMark keys="G5">Selected: fully lit</KeyMark>
  <KeyMark keys="r3c1">This step's note: fully lit</KeyMark>
  <KeyMark keys="PADS">Other keys: half lit</KeyMark>
  <Caption>Step 5 selected: its column is highlighted and its note lights up fully on the pads.</Caption>
</Shot>

The notes of the selected steps light up fully on the pads; while playing with no step selected, those of the step under the playhead do. Once a step is selected:

<Strip>
  <Step keys="G5" shot="seq-selected" at="76,15,14,96">Tap GRID 5: step 5 is selected and its column highlighted.</Step>
  <Step keys="OK, OK" shot="seq-lane" at="footer"><Key k="OK" /> cycles what you edit, **NOTE → LEN → VEL → TIME**; the bottom line shows the lane and its value.</Step>
  <Step keys="ADD" shot="seq-lane" at="footer"><Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> change it; held they repeat. <Key k="ESC" /> clears the selection.</Step>
</Strip>

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| PADS | put that pad's notes into the selected steps, or take them out: a chord goes in or out as one group (a step that holds any of its notes loses them all and gains none); while playing they edit without sounding |
| <Key k="OK" /> | what you edit: NOTE (pitch) → LEN (length) → VEL (velocity) → TIME (small timing offset) |
| <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> | change it; held they repeat; with <Key k="FN" /> in bigger steps |
| <Key k="FN+OK" /> | switch between editing all notes of the chord (ALL) and just one of them (its name on the bottom line) |
| <Key k="ESC" /> | clear the selection (the next ESC changes scene) |

On a drum track the pads choose the drum, so the NOTE lane does not move hits (ADD / SUB say PADS PICK DRUMS); LEN, VEL and TIME work as on any track.

<Keywords k="edit notes, pitch, note length, velocity, timing, nudge, microtiming, select steps, hold step, tap step, 编辑音符" />

## DRUMS: the drum machine {#seq-drums}

On a drum track (<Track n="1" /> by default) the STEP view is the drum machine, its tab reads <Page name="DRUMS" />, and it is where drum tracks start. <Key k="OK" /> switches to ROLL, a piano roll of the drums in use, as on any track.

<Shot name="seq-drums">
  <Mark at="10,14,96,108">Eight drums × 16 steps</Mark>
  <Mark at="110,14,119,108">The chosen drum (CH) on the GRID</Mark>
  <Mark at="footer">DRUMS</Mark>
  <KeyMark keys="G1 G3 G5">Has hits: half lit</KeyMark>
  <KeyMark keys="G2">Nothing on this step: off</KeyMark>
  <Caption>The drum machine on T1: the beat of every drum on the left, the chosen drum's 16 steps on the right.</Caption>
</Shot>

- **PADS** are drums and velocities: each column is a drum, each row how hard (top row 127, then 100, 70, bottom row 40). A pad sounds and chooses the drum the GRID on the right shows.
- **Hold a pad and tap steps**: that drum goes into them as hard as the pad's row (or comes out where it already is). Or tap a step first and then press pads.
- **Change a hit**: select its step (hold or tap it), <Key k="OK" /> to VEL or TIME, <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" />.
- How hard a hit is shows as dither density in four clear levels (solid, half, a quarter, an eighth); a hit off the beat is a thin bar at its place in the step.
- While you hold a pad its drum's row lights up across, as dense as the pad hits, and its name turns inverse.

<Strip>
  <Step keys="hold r3c3~CH + G1, G3, G5" shot="seq-drums" at="110,14,119,108">Hold the closed hat (CH, bottom row) and tap every other step: CH goes in at the softest velocity.</Step>
  <Step keys="hold G1 + OK, OK, ADD" shot="seq-drum-edit" at="footer">Hold step 1, tap <Key k="OK" /> twice for VEL and press <Key k="ADD" />: its hits get harder. Let go and step 1 is no longer selected.</Step>
</Strip>

- GRID lights as on every note page: steps with hits half lit, selected steps fully lit, empty steps off; while you hold pads, the steps that hold their drums are fully lit.
- Record live and the RECORD panel work as on the other tracks.

|---|---|

- Setting cells: a tap switches them; <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> work too.

- GRID lights: settings half lit, REC and SNAP fully lit when on, the focused cell fully lit, takes half lit.

## Capture: get back what you just played {#capture}

<Icon name="capture" /> No need to turn on REC: every note you play on the pads is kept in the background (capture). When something good came out, put it into the sequencer afterwards:

<Strip>
  <Step keys="r3c1~C, r3c3~E, r3c5~G" shot="seq-keyboard">Just play. A pause longer than 2.5 s, a track change or 60 seconds starts a new take.</Step>
  <Step keys="hold OK" shot="record" at="cells:2,1-3,4">Hold <Key k="OK" />: the two middle rows are the last 8 takes, with number, track and note count.</Step>
  <Step keys="G6" shot="record" at="cell:2,2 cells:4,1-4,4">Tap a take: row 4 shows how its bar would land. <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> pick another bar.</Step>
  <Step keys="OK" shot="record-placed" at="14,94,212,28"><Key k="OK" />: into a **new pattern** on this track; not a single existing note is touched.</Step>
</Strip>

- The 8 takes share room for 400 notes; when it is full, the oldest take makes way.
- Without a free pattern slot it tells you; use <Key k="FN+OK" /> to merge into the current pattern instead.
- Takes played while the transport ran keep their place against the bar; takes played while stopped start with their first note, timed at the BPM of that moment.
- With SNAP on (default) every note lands on the nearest whole step with a whole-step length; off, your playing's fine timing is kept.

## Long patterns and step pages {#seq-long}

A pattern can be up to 256 steps long; the GRID and the screen always show one page of 16 steps. The page number sits at the right end of the bottom line (for example `2/4`, yellow when you turned the page yourself).

<Shot name="fn-held">
  <Mark at="cells:3,1-3,2">PAGE − / PAGE +: page / pages</Mark>
  <Mark at="cells:3,3-3,4">LEN − / LEN +: the length</Mark>
  <Caption>The SEQ menu (hold FN): row 3 turns the pages and sets the length.</Caption>
</Shot>

- **Set the length**: STEPS on the TRACK page (<Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> one step, <Key k="FN+ADD" /> / <Key k="FN+SUB" /> to the next page boundary), or LEN − / LEN + in the FN menu: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 48 … 256.
- **Turn the page**: <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> on the note page with no step selected (their icons are → ←, and PAGE n/m pops up), or PAGE − / PAGE + in the FN menu.
- **Change pattern**: <Key k="FN+ADD" /> / <Key k="FN+SUB" /> on the note page with no step selected go to the next / previous pattern (PATTERN n/m and its name pop up); while playing the switch waits for the end of the loop (NEXT PATTERN).
- While playing the GRID follows the playhead from page to page; after you turn a page yourself it stays there until the next PLAY. With a step selected it does not turn.
- A new pattern starts with the length of the current one; a duplicated pattern keeps its length. Steps beyond the length are off on the GRID.
- Memory is used only by pages that hold notes. When it runs out the note is not written and the screen says MEMORY FULL - NOTE NOT WRITTEN.

## Copy, paste, clear, quantize {#seq-tools}

Hold <Key k="FN" /> with a letter, or pick the item in the FN menu (COPY, PASTE, CLEAR, QUANT, DUP: their second line says STEPS or PATTERN). On the note page they act on the selected steps; with none selected, and on PATTERN and TRACK, on the whole pattern:

  <Group keys="V">Paste</Group>

  <Group keys="Q">Quantize</Group>
  <Group keys="D">Duplicate pattern</Group>
  <Group keys="T">Tap tempo</Group>
  <Caption>Edit keys while FN is held</Caption>
</Keymap>

| Keys | Does |
|---|---|
| <Key k="FN+C" /> / <Key k="FN+V" /> | copy / paste (PASTE is off until something is copied) |
| <Key k="FN+X" /> | clear (asks first for a whole pattern) |
| <Key k="FN+Q" /> | quantize to whole steps |
| <Key k="FN+D" /> | duplicate the pattern into the next free slot |
| <Key k="FN+T" /> | tap tempo |

The SEQ menu on the GRID, the same on all three pages:

| Row | Items |
|---|---|
| 1 | COPY · PASTE · CLEAR · QUANT |
| 2 | DUP · VIEW (ROLL / STEP) · RECORD (the RECORD panel) · SCOPE (<Key k="FN+OK" />, with steps selected) |
| 3 | PAGE − · PAGE + (note page, long patterns) · LEN − · LEN + |
| 4 | SAVE · TAP · REC · SETUP, as on every screen |

More on the menu in [The menu on each screen](./fn.mdx#fn-menus).

:::warning

There is no undo, so clearing a whole pattern always asks first.

:::
