# 1 INSTR

INSTR is the current track's sound: which engine, which sound (preset), every parameter of the engine and the track's effects. Each of the four tracks uses one engine and remembers which page it was on.

| Engine | Default track | Pages (<Key k="CTRL" /> steps) |
|---|---|---|
| DRUMS drum machine | <Track n="1" /> | VOICE, KIT, FX |
| JUNO analog synth | <Track n="2" /> | VOICE, CHORD, ARP, DCO, VCF, ENV, LFO, FX |
| DX7 FM synth | <Track n="3" /> | VOICE, CHORD, ARP, ALGO, LEVELS, OP1–OP6, LFO, FX |
| SAMPLER | <Track n="4" /> | VOICE, CHORD, ARP, SAMPLE, TONE, FX |

The pages run in signal order: the sound (VOICE), the track's MIDI effects (CHORD, ARP: the keys go through them first), the engine's parameter pages, then the effects (FX). When the tabs do not fit, the bottom line shows the ones around the current page with `<` `>` and "page / total" on the right; hold <Key k="CTRL" /> for the map of every page.

- <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" />: straight to the sound above / below in the list (ADD goes up, as in the browser), ready to hear. With <Key k="FN" /> held they jump 10 sounds.
- <Key k="OK" />: opens the sound browser.
- Saving lives in the FN menu: on every INSTR page it has PRESETS, SAVE, SAVE AS and ENGINE, so you can browse and save without going back to VOICE (see [The menu on each screen](./fn.mdx#fn-menus)).

Changing the engine gives the track the new engine's default sound and goes back to VOICE; the track's sequence, chord and ARP are not touched.

## Browsing and saving sounds {#instr-presets}

<Strip>
  <Step keys="G2" shot="preset-browser" at="0,13,240,10">PRESET: the sound list, with "position / total" at the top right.</Step>
  <Step keys="FN + F, B, A, S, S, OK" shot="preset-filtered" at="0,13,240,10"><Key k="FN+F" /> opens the search field: type (for example `BASS`) and press <Key k="OK" />; the list keeps only the matching sounds.</Step>
  <Step keys="OK" shot="instr-voice" at="8,40,200,26"><Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> move, <Key k="OK" /> loads.</Step>
</Strip>

- In the list the pads and the GRID do not type, so a stray key never starts a filter: searching is always <Key k="FN+F" />. An empty search shows everything again.
- <Key k="ESC" /> clears the filter first, a second press closes.
- <Key k="ADD" /> moves up, <Key k="SUB" /> down; <Key k="FN" /> + <Key k=";" /> <Key k="." /> too. <Key k="FN+ADD" /> / <Key k="FN+SUB" /> move 8 rows at a time.
- **SAVE** (FN menu): your own sound is overwritten; a factory sound is stored as a new sound of yours with the next free number (for example `LEAD I 2`), without asking. **SAVE AS** asks for a name. Your sounds live on the SD card, one bank per engine (`CardenzaSynth/Presets/<engine>/`).

<Keywords k="presets, preset, preset library, browse, search, search sound, find, filter, fn f, save sound, save as, patch library, 音色库" />

## Parameter pages {#instr-params}

The engine's other pages (and CHORD and ARP) are parameter pages: **each GRID key picks one parameter**, <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> change it (held they repeat, with <Key k="FN" /> in big steps), <Key k="FN+OK" /> (or RESET in the FN menu) restores its default. Switches and choices step with a second press on the same key, or with <Key k="OK" />.

<Shot name="instr-juno-vcf">

  <Mark at="cell:1,2">RESO</Mark>
  <Mark at="cell:1,3">ENV</Mark>
  <Mark at="cell:1,4">POL</Mark>
  <Mark at="cells:3,1-4,4">The group's picture: filter curve</Mark>
  <KeyMark keys="G1">Focus: fully lit</KeyMark>
  <KeyMark keys="G2-G6">Parameters: half lit</KeyMark>
  <KeyMark keys="G7-G16">Empty cells: off</KeyMark>
  <KeyMark keys="ADD SUB">Change value</KeyMark>
  <Caption>Juno VCF page: parameters on the top two rows, the filter curve below.</Caption>
</Shot>

Cells without a parameter draw the group's picture: envelopes, filter curves, algorithms, waveforms, a scope… Every parameter's range, default and options are in the engine references below.

**Hold a parameter cell** for a moment and the pads become its values: one pad per value (or the range spread evenly over the pads), the current value fully lit. Press a pad to jump there; the value pops up at the bottom of the screen. Let go of the cell and the pads play again. A short tap still selects the cell as before.

<Keywords k="parameter, parameters, edit sound, knob, tweak, default, reset, hold cell, pick value, 参数" />

## CHORD and ARP pages {#instr-midifx}

Right after VOICE, every melodic engine has the track's two MIDI effects: **CHORD** (one key plays a chord) and **ARP** (held keys play one after another). They belong to the track, not to the sound: changing sound or engine keeps them, and they are saved with the project. Drum tracks have neither. How they work: [Chords](./play.mdx#play-chords) and [Arpeggiator](./play.mdx#play-arp).
