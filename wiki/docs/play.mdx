# Playing

The PADS always play the current track. Keyboard layout, scale, root, octave, velocity and scale lock are shared by all four tracks and set on the **KEYS** page of <Scene name="SETUP" /> (see [KEYS](./setup.mdx#setup-keys)). Chords and the arpeggiator (ARP) belong to each track: every melodic track has its own **CHORD** and **ARP** pages in <Scene name="INSTR" />. All of them are saved with the project; NEW starts from PIANO, C MAJOR, no chords and every ARP off.

| Layout | Arrangement | Lights |
|---|---|---|
| **PIANO** (default) | two whole octaves: the lower two rows from C3 at the bottom left, the upper two an octave up; in each pair the white keys C D E F G A B C below and the black keys above. The gaps (before C#, between E and F, after A#) are silent | gaps <Led state="off" /> |
| **SCALE** | only the notes of the scale, from the root left to right up the scale, each row one octave up. No wrong notes | all <Led state="half" /> |
| **CHROMA** | one semitone per key from the root, left to right and bottom to top | all <Led state="half" /> |

## Velocity {#play-velocity}

The pads are not velocity sensitive; every key of a melodic track plays at the same velocity: **VELO** on the KEYS page (1–127, default 110). Notes recorded into the sequencer get it too, and you can change it step by step on the note page later (VEL, see [Selecting and editing](./seq.mdx#seq-edit)). On a drum track the row sets how hard instead (see [Drums](#play-drums)).

<Keywords k="velocity, dynamics, how hard, loudness of a note, 力度" />

## Scale lock {#play-lock}

In the PIANO and CHROMA layouts, what keys outside the scale do:

| LOCK | Keys outside the scale | Light |
|---|---|---|
| **OFF** | sound as usual | <Led state="half" /> |
| **MUTE** | are silent | <Led state="off" /> |
| **SNAP** | play the nearest scale note below | <Led state="half" /> |

## Chords {#play-chords}

The **CHORD** page in <Scene name="INSTR" /> turns one key into a chord. Each track has its own chord; it stays when you change the sound or the engine. Drum tracks have no CHORD page.

<Strip>
  <Step keys="hold CTRL + G2" shot="instr-chord" at="cell:1,1">In <Scene name="INSTR" /> hold <Key k="CTRL" /> and press G2: the track's CHORD page. CHORD is OFF.</Step>
  <Step keys="G1" shot="chord-on" at="cell:1,1 9,43,170,20">Tap CHORD: TRIAD. The picture shows which notes the key on the root plays (C3 PLAYS C3 E3 G3).</Step>
  <Step keys="hold r3c1~C" shot="chord-held" at="cells:3,1-4,4">Press a key: three notes sound together, and the chord tones light up on the screen and on the pads.</Step>
</Strip>

- **CHORD**: OFF / TRIAD / 7TH / MAJ / MIN / SUS4 / MAJ7 / MIN7 / DOM7 / POWER / OCTAVE. TRIAD and 7TH stack thirds in the current scale (keys outside it borrow a major triad / dominant seventh); the others are fixed chords.
- **VOICING**: ROOT (root position) / INV1 (first inversion: lowest note up an octave) / INV2 (second inversion: the two lowest notes up an octave) / SPREAD (second note up an octave).
- The second line shows the triad on every degree of the key (the font is upper case only: minor is written `D-`, diminished `BDIM`).
- Recorded live, chord tones are separate notes, and the ARP plays them one by one. On the SEQ note page a key's chord goes into a step, or out of it, as one group (see [Selecting and editing](./seq.mdx#seq-edit)).

## Arpeggiator {#play-arp}

The **ARP** page in <Scene name="INSTR" />: hold a few keys and they sound one after another. Every melodic track has its own ARP with its own settings, and several can run at once (a HOLD arpeggio on T2 keeps going while you play T3).

<Strip>
  <Step keys="hold CTRL + G3" shot="instr-arp" at="cell:1,1">In <Scene name="INSTR" /> hold <Key k="CTRL" /> and press G3: the track's ARP page. ARP OFF dims the picture.</Step>
  <Step keys="G1, hold r3c1~C + r3c3~E + r3c5~G" shot="arp-running" at="cells:3,1-4,4">Tap ARP: UP. Hold C E G: the notes play in turn from low to high.</Step>
</Strip>

  <Mark at="cell:2,1">HOLD: keeps going after release</Mark>

  <KeyMark keys="r3c1 r3c3 r3c5">Held keys</KeyMark>
  <Caption>ARP UP: holding C E G, the three notes play in turn from low to high. Each bar is a step at its pitch, as wide as GATE; the white bar is the step sounding now.</Caption>
</Shot>

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| ARP | OFF / UP / DOWN / UP-DN / RANDOM / ORDER (in the order pressed) |
| RATE | 1/4, 1/8, 1/8T, 1/16 (default), 1/16T, 1/32 |
| RANGE | 1–4 octaves (upwards) |
| GATE | 10–100 % (how much of each note value sounds, default 50 %) |
| HOLD | keeps going after you let go; pressing again after releasing all starts a new set. One tap on the cell switches it, no need to select it first |

- Held keys (every tone of a chord counts) sound one at a time in order, up to 16 notes. The first note sounds at once.
- Stopped, it runs freely at the BPM; playing, it locks to the beat (1/16 follows swing), and PLAY / stop never breaks its rhythm.
- UP / DOWN / UP-DN continue from the last note played and never repeat at the turns.
- With REC on while playing, the ARP's notes are recorded into its track's pattern with the GATE length.
- Changing track does not stop the other tracks' ARPs. Turning HOLD off stops it at once (only keys still held keep playing). <Key k="FN+ESC" /> (stop all) and loading a project stop every ARP.

## Drums {#play-drums}

On a drum track (<Track n="1" /> by default) each pad **column** is one drum and the **row** is how hard: the top row hits hardest (velocity 127), then 100, 70, and the bottom row softest (40). At rest the pads are half lit; a held pad lights its column from the bottom up to its row, and a hit runs along the column, further the harder it is. On screen the pads show how hard by dither density, in four clear levels. Drums do not arpeggiate and ignore scale, chords and VELO. Each drum's level is on the INSTR KIT page (see [DRUMS](./instr.mdx#engine-drums)).
