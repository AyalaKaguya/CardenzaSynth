# Sampling

<Icon name="sample" /> The SAMPLER engine (<Track n="4" /> by default) plays a WAV recording. Load samples from the SD card or record one with the built-in microphone. All apps share the `CardenzaSynth/Samples` folder on the card.

Supported files: mono 16-bit PCM WAV, 8–96 kHz sample rate, up to 5 minutes. Samples up to 2 seconds are kept in memory; longer ones play straight from the card.

<Keywords k="sample, sampling, sampler, microphone, record audio, wav, field recording, vocals, 采样" />

## Loading a sample {#sampler-load}

<Strip>
  <Step keys="hold TAB + G4, hold CTRL + G4" shot="instr-sampler-sample" at="cell:1,1">In <Scene name="INSTR" /> switch to <Track n="4" /> and go to the SAMPLE page (hold <Key k="CTRL" />, press G4).</Step>
  <Step keys="G1" shot="sampler-files" at="0,13,240,10">FILE opens the sample list; <Key k="FN+F" /> searches it.</Step>
  <Step keys="OK" shot="instr-sampler-sample" at="cells:1,3-1,4"><Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> choose, <Key k="OK" /> loads, <Key k="ESC" /> goes back.</Step>
</Strip>

On every page of a SAMPLER track the FN menu also has SAMPLES (the list), RECORD and PREVIEW (plays the track's sample at its original pitch). Once loaded, the pads play the sample; ROOT decides which key plays it at its original pitch.

:::tip

In the web simulator "Load WAV…" puts a WAV from your computer into the shared sample folder (see [Storage and samples](./web.mdx#web-storage)).

:::

## Recording a sample {#sampler-record}

<Strip>
  <Step keys="G2" shot="sampler-rec" at="8,32,222,34">REC on the SAMPLE page (or RECORD in the FN menu) opens the recorder; READY means you can start. Up to 60 seconds.</Step>
  <Step keys="OK" shot="sampler-rec" at="8,72,222,12"><Key k="OK" /> starts, <Key k="OK" /> again stops. The input level shows while recording.</Step>
</Strip>

- With a card the take goes straight to a file on it (`CardenzaSynth/Session/take-<track>.wav`), safe on the card about once a second: a power cut loses at most the last second. A full card stops the recording and keeps what is there (CARD FULL). Without a card the take fits in free memory, only a few seconds.
- The microphone shares a clock pin with the speaker: while recording the speaker is silent; the sequencer keeps running.
- The microphone only runs while the recorder is on screen: leaving it (another scene or track) cancels the recording. <Key k="ESC" /> while recording cancels it too.
- An unsaved take survives a power cut or a switch to another app: when the app starts again the recorder opens with it (UNSAVED TAKE IS BACK).

:::warning

An unsaved take is never lost by accident: REC asks RECORD AGAIN? before replacing it, and <Key k="ESC" /> in the trimmer asks DISCARD TAKE? (<Key k="OK" /> = yes, <Key k="ESC" /> = no).

:::

<Keywords k="record sample, microphone, mic, record audio, vocals, take, unsaved take, power cut, 录采样" />

## Trimming and previewing {#sampler-trim}

After recording you are in the trimmer:

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <Key k="G1" /> / <Key k="G2" /> (or <Key k="OK" />) | pick the start / end marker (the chosen one fully lit); <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> move it (hold <Key k="FN" /> for bigger steps) |
| <Key k="G3" /> or <Key k="PLAY" /> | preview |
| <Key k="G4" /> | SAVE: saves the take at once as the next free `TAKE_nn` and loads it on the track |

## Saving, renaming, deleting {#sampler-save}

- Trimmer cell 4 (SAVE) saves the take as a WAV named `TAKE_01`, `TAKE_02`… (the first free number), without asking for a name.
- In the sample list <Key k="FN+OK" /> opens RENAME / DELETE for the file under the cursor. DELETE asks first and removes the WAV from the card.
- A sample another track is using cannot be deleted (FILE IN USE).
- Sample files belong to the shared folder, not to a project; a project only remembers which file it uses.
