# Web simulator

<Icon name="web" /> Without a device, use it right in the browser: [cardenza-synth.203.io](https://cardenza-synth.203.io/) (or [cardenza-synth.vercel.app](https://cardenza-synth.vercel.app/)). It is not a look-alike but the same firmware code (the groovebox interface, the AMY engines, the sequencer, the storage protocol) compiled to WebAssembly: screen, key lights and sound are the same as on the device.

<Keywords k="simulator, emulator, web, browser, online, try it, try it live, no device, 模拟器" />

## Connect your Cardenza (USB) {#web-usb}

<Icon name="usb" /> Plug the Cardenza into the computer with a USB cable (Chrome or Edge) and open the [guide](/). There is no button to press: the guide's pages ask the browser whether they may use MIDI devices; allow it, and from then on the device connects as soon as it is plugged in.

- The device follows the lessons: it goes to the lesson's screen, the keys to press blink (the others dim) until you get it right, then stay lit; the page says so, try a few more keys, then press Next.
- Your own work is not touched: when a lesson starts, the device keeps your working state on the SD card and plays in a practice project; when the guide ends (you leave the lessons, close the page, unplug the cable, or 30 seconds pass without a word from the page) it comes back as it was. The guide needs an SD card in the device.
- The manual pages do not drive the device; it keeps running as usual.
- Unplug the cable to disconnect; plug it back in and it reconnects.
- It talks to the Cardenza over USB MIDI (Web MIDI, SysEx included): no driver is needed, and USB MIDI and USB audio keep working. If you blocked it before, allow MIDI devices in the site settings (left of the address bar) and reload.

## Which computer key is which {#web-keys}

<Icon name="keyboard" /> The computer keyboard maps to the device's 4×14 keys by **physical position**; every key cap shows its computer key, and the key on the page lights up when pressed:

<Keymap>
  <Group keys="r0c1-r0c12">Number row 1 … =</Group>
  <Group keys="r1c1-r1c12">The Q row</Group>
  <Group keys="r2c1-r2c12">The A row</Group>
  <Group keys="r3c1-r3c12">The Z row</Group>
  <Group keys="ESC TAB FN CTRL">Esc · Tab · Caps Lock / left Shift · Ctrl</Group>
  <Group keys="ADD SUB OK PLAY">Backspace · \\ · Enter · Space</Group>
  <Caption>The computer keyboard's four rows are the device's four rows</Caption>
</Keymap>

| Device | Computer |
|---|---|
| <Key k="ESC" /> <Key k="TAB" /> <Key k="FN" /> <Key k="CTRL" /> | Esc / Tab / Caps Lock or left Shift / Ctrl |
| <Key k="ADD" /> <Key k="SUB" /> <Key k="OK" /> <Key k="PLAY" /> | Backspace / `\` / Enter / Space |
| HOME side key | Home |
| the pads without a letter | right Shift (row 3) · left / right Alt (row 4) |

- <Key k="ESC" /> is also the backquote key (left of 1). On macOS Caps Lock latches <Key k="FN" /> (press it again to release it); hold left Shift for a <Key k="FN" /> that lets go with the key.
- You can also click or touch the keys on the page, several at once on a touch screen. A right-click latches a key down (to hold ESC, FN… while you click the GRID); right-click it again to release it.

<Keywords k="keyboard mapping, computer keyboard, which key, key bindings, shortcuts, caps lock, mac, right click, latch, 键位映射" />

## Sound {#web-audio}

<Icon name="sound" /> Browsers only play sound after a click on the page: the first time, click anywhere (or "Start audio"). The sound is synthesized live by the same audio engine; latency depends on the browser and computer.

<Keywords k="no sound, audio, start audio, sound not working, latency, 没声音" />

## Storage and samples {#web-storage}

- Settings, projects and sounds are stored in the browser (IndexedDB) in the same folders as on the SD card (`CardenzaSynth/…`), and survive a reload. Clearing the site's data deletes them.
- The working state that the device keeps by itself ([Your work is kept](./projects.mdx#project-session)) is kept by the browser too: it is written as you go and when the page is hidden, and is still there after a reload or after closing the page, like pulling the power on the device. To keep it as a project, save with <Key k="FN+S" /> as usual.
- On the simulator page, "Load project…" copies `.ccproj` files from a Cardenza's card into the browser (never over an existing project, a name already taken gets a number) and opens the first one; the others are in SETUP FILES.
- "Load WAV…" puts audio files from your computer into the shared sample folder `Samples/`, where the SAMPLER's file list finds them. Anything that is not a mono 16-bit WAV (stereo, 24-bit, MP3…) is converted to mono 16-bit 44.1 kHz and cut to 5 seconds.
